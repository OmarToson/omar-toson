01 · About

From AR real estate apps to city-scale digital twins

I'm a Senior Software Engineer with over 6 years of experience in 3D application development, Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality and Digital Twin solutions. I build immersive, real-time 3D environments in Unity for infrastructure, real estate, IoT integrations and simulation-based systems.

I model and optimize my own assets in Autodesk 3ds Max, bring geospatial data into 3D scenes for location-based analysis, and design Digital Twins that support simulation, monitoring and scenario-based decisions.

I also work across the enterprise side: ArcGIS platform concepts, enterprise databases, web service integration and delivery in structured SDLC and Agile teams. Today I lead the 3D Geospatial System Development team at ALKAN and deliver technical training for government bodies.
